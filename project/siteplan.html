Site Name:

Who is Dude Dans – from ©PersonaQuery. I chose this name because it is simple and immediately gets the point of the webpage across to the user. I added the fictional company, “PersonaQuery” because I felt that the website being Who is Dude Dans doesn’t sound like a company, brand, or group. But adding the fictional company makes it sound more professional and realistic.

Site Purpose:

The purpose of this site is to enlighten people on who the popular youtube animator Dude Dans is. This site will include all that you need to know about who he is and what he does. Along with fun facts about how he started, and how he does what he does.

Scenarios:

  • How did he start out?

  • What programs does he use to make his animations?

  • Where can I go to buy his new merchandise?
